The AI Development Bootcamp lasts 6 months.
Students study 10 hours per week.
The bootcamp teaches HTML, CSS, JavaScript and GitHub.
Python and FastAPI are used for backend development.
RAG means Retrieval-Augmented Generation.
RAG retrieves relevant information before asking an LLM to answer.
Embeddings convert text into numerical vectors.
FAISS is used to store and search vectors.
Ollama allows language models to run locally.
Students learn Docker and deployment basics.
